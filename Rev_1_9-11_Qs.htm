Questions Revelation is asking me ...
  • John was a 'brother' who was close enough to 'share ... tribulation,
         the kingdom & the patient endurance' with these churches.
         Am I close enough to anyone to share these things with anyone?
         How could I build those kinds of relationships with my brothers &
         sisters in Christ? Especially those in 'tribulation'?

  • John experienced 'tribulation' ... even his being on Patmos was
         'on account of the word of God & the testimony of Jesus'.
         What risks do I take in trying to be a 'faithful witness' to
         the word of God & the Gospel of Jesus?
         What is one thing I could do this week to be a more faithful witness
         to Jesus as a habit in my life?

  • Despite his circumstances, John was 'in the Spirit'.
         What stops me from being 'in the Spirit' day by day?
         What disciplines could I strengthen to more strongly live 'in the Spirit'
         as a way of life?

  • ... ?
