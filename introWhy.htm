Why a "hitchhiker's guide"?!
How do I use this guide?
“DON'T PANIC” — The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy by Douglas Adams (1979)

This online resource for exploring John's Apocalypse in the NT is framed as a 'hitchhiker's guide' for a number of reasons:

  • A hitchhiker is more of a traveller than a tourist;
  • Exploring the various layers of history, culture, language & geography of these unfamiliar lands, cities & writings — especially in the Apocalypse — can be very daunting & even disorienting … an accessible informative guide can help us keep our bearings!
  • I remember thinking, "I wish there was a guide like that so ordinary people - who don't have a theological degree or $5000 worth of textbooks - could still read & understand the Apocalypse enough to hear the music of Creation, Redemption & Renewal ... & to see how we can start joining in now!"
  • I recall reading The Hitchhikers' Guide to the Galaxy by Douglas Adams (written long before the internet & hand-held digital devices were common things) & often thought the concept would be very cool for travellers in the biblical lands & writings!

My goal is to produce a guide that allows fellow-'travellers' to understand what you are reading & seeing as you make your way slowly through each passge & section of John's Apocalypse, to 'see' what was being revealed to those first readers & to be ready to hear — &, God-willing, even to answer — the questions that the Apocalypse will be asking us.
