How do I use this guide?
What approach … ?
“Whoever looks intently into the perfect law that gives freedom, and continues in it — not forgetting what they have heard, but doing it — they will be blessed in what they do.” - James, the brother of Jesus (1:22-25)

This 'hitchhiker's guide' aims to achieve a number of goals:

  • To provide easy & immediate access to literary, cultural & historical information that helps a modern reader to understand (as much as possible) what each significant word, phrase, sentence &/or passage would have communicated to an original reader in their context:
    • the general background information is provided in the 'introduction' pages, particularly those on the Jewish, Roman & Christian contexts of the Apocalypse
    • information relevant to each specific passage in Revelation is available through links on the relevant page within the guide.
  • To prompt & guide readers through a process of contemplative reading, understanding & digesting what each passage reveals:
    • the text is broken out of the usual 'verse & passage' formats in modern translations;
    • visual mapping is used to lay out key ideas & connections so that repeated &/or related concepts/assertions are aligned &/or grouped;
    • room is provided to allow notes (from the comments &/or our own thoughts & insights) to be easily added.
  • Once a reader has lingered long enough to understand the meanings & implications of the passage, they are encouraged to consider the 'questions Revelation is asking me' … this is really where we move from gaining information & insights to 'hearing what the Spirit is saying to the churches' & growing in wisdom!
On each page there is also a link at the bottom where readers can send 'feedback &/or suggestions' for how each page could be improved. All feedback, suggestions & even criticisms are very welcome! My hope is that this guide may be as useful as possible & I'd be glad to receive your thoughts re how to make it excellent. 🙂
