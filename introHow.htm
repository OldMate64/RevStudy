What approach … ?
“Whoever looks intently into the perfect law that gives freedom, and continues in it — not forgetting what they have heard, but doing it — they will be blessed in what they do.” - James, the brother of Jesus (James 1:22-25)

This 'hitchhiker's guide' aims to achieve a number of goals for individuals & groups setting out on an unhurried exploration of the Apocalypse. Here are my suggestions for how to make the most of it:

  • Getting started:
    • Deliberately & consciously choose to put aside any pre-existing assumptions & even feelings you have previously developed regarding the Apoocalypse;
    • Read the whole Revelation in 1 sitting at least once … it should take less than 90 minutes even if you read it aloud, which the author seems to encourage! (cf. 1:3)
    • Read & explore the 'introduction' pages, particularly those on the various cultural contexts of the Apocalypse;
    • Take some unhurried time to look over the whole outline of the Apocalypse a few times to get a sense of the narrative arc, the major characters, the various scenes, the repeating themes & the overall structure … this will help keep your bearings when you focus on specific passage.
    • Begin at the start & move forward (without hurrying!) from there … resist the temptation of skipping ahead to more 'exciting' sections! To understand this revelation, this 'apocalypse', rightly means reading it as the author clearly intended.
  • When contemplating each passage:
    • Navigate to the page for the section you are up to, using the links in the Outline … you will see the text is formatted in a 'visual' way.
    • Select the 'download passage' link to access a PDF version of the formatted text … you can print this off to make your own notes (or annotate the PDF directly on your device, if you prefer). You will see that room is provided to allow notes (from the comments &/or your own thoughts & insights) to be easily added. On some pages there will also be an 'example annotation' link to an example of my annotations for that passage. Of course, this is just provided as a model for how to go about this process, not as something you should just copy without 'chewing your own food'!
    • Once you have lingered long enough to understand (at least mostly!) the meanings & implications of the passage, you are encouraged to consider the 'questions Revelation is asking me' … this is really where we move from gaining information & insights to 'hearing what the Spirit is saying to the churches' & growing in wisdom!
    • Before moving on, if you have any feedback &/or suggestions for how each page could be improved, please use the link at the bottom of the page to send your thoughts to me. This is a 'living' site which I expect to be constantly improving, so all feedback, suggestions & even criticisms are very welcome! My hope is that this guide may become increasingly useful to whoever uses it & I'd be glad to receive your thoughts re how to make it excellent. 🙂
