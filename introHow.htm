This 'hitchhiker's guide' aims to achieve a number of goals for individuals & groups setting out on an unhurried exploration of the Apocalypse. Here are my suggestions for how to make the most of it:

  • Getting started:
    • Put aside any pre-existing assumptions & feelings you have regarding the Apoocalypse.
    • Read the whole Revelation in 1 sitting at least once.
    • Read & explore the 'introduction' pages.
    • Look over the whole outline of the Apocalypse a few time.
    • Begin at the start & move forward (without hurrying!) from there.
  • When contemplating each passage:
    • Navigate to the page for the section you are up to, using the links in the Outline.
    • Read the text a few times & notice how it is formatted in a 'visual' way.
    • Select the 'download passage' link to access a PDF version of the formatted text.
    • Select the highlighted words/phrases to access specific information regarding those term
    • Linger in contemplating these verses & the information provided long enough to understand their original meanings (at least mostly!). Annotate the passage PDF to capture & clarify your thoughts.
    • Consider the 'questions Revelation is asking me'.
    • Before moving on, please give any feedback &/or suggestions you would like!
